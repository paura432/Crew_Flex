## 2. Content summary per section

**Cover (p.1).** One-sentence summary: first build "una herramienta útil para captar y ordenar perfiles
técnicos de mantenimiento aeronáutico"; then that base lets companies cover "trabajos temporales, refuerzos,
AOG, campañas y vacantes especializadas" through "una vía rápida, filtrada y segura".

**1. Resumen ejecutivo (p.2).** JobSlice Aero is a specialised platform for aircraft maintenance. Phase 1
must not be presented "únicamente como una bolsa de empleo" but as a daily-utility tool: a "pasaporte
profesional digital" (experience, licences, courses, documents, availability, preferences, personal
logbook). Business goal: build a technician base "suficientemente amplia, ordenada y accionable" so that in
phase 2 companies publish needs and reach matching profiles. Differentiator vs LinkedIn/InfoJobs/agencies
is specialisation: "licencia, tipo de avión, motor, cursos recurrentes, disponibilidad real, movilidad,
tarifa y privacidad". Thesis: "Sin técnicos no hay marketplace." Five elements: Pasaporte técnico,
Herramientas diarias, Modo trabajo extra ("próximos días, fines de semana, rotaciones o AOG"), Marketplace
B2B ("candidatos prefiltrados mediante Candidate Pack"), Privacidad ("privado, anónimo, visible a empresas
verificadas o abierto a oportunidades").

**2. Problema (p.2–3).** Technicians: experience scattered across CV, certificates, WhatsApp, company
folders; multi-country/base/contract careers; generic CVs hide aircraft/engine/licence/courses; wish to
look for extra work "sin exponerse públicamente ni levantar sospechas en su empresa actual"; opportunities
flow through informal contacts and recruiters. Companies: HR and maintenance waste time filtering; needs are
very specific and urgent ("B1/B2, A320/B737, line/base, EWIS, FTS, Human Factors, disponibilidad inmediata,
movilidad y permiso de trabajo"); speed of contact is critical for short/urgent needs; generalist portals do
not understand the aviation taxonomy.

**3. Visión de producto (p.3).** Definition: "pasaporte profesional digital y marketplace especializado".
"Dos productos en uno": a technical tool for the technician (goal: "registro y uso recurrente") and a labour
marketplace for the company (revenue "por publicación, acceso, suscripción o contratación").

**4. Fase 1: captar técnicos (p.3–4).** Priority is technician acquisition with a practical and economic
reason to sign up. Recommended message: "Crea tu pasaporte profesional aeronáutico… y activa oportunidades
compatibles cuando quieras trabajar extra." Channels: WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn (B1/B2, sheet metal,
structures, avionics, recruiters), Instagram/TikTok short content, Part-147 schools, referrals with a
"badge de founding member", events/fairs with QR. Why a technician registers: ordered documents; CV and
Candidate Pack without re-filling forms; discreet extra-work availability; compatible offers; privacy
control (hide current employer, appear anonymously).

**5. Producto para técnicos (p.4–5).** 5.1 Onboarding in 8 steps: role (B1, B2, Cat A, certificador, sheet
metal, estructuras, aviónica, motores, cabina, trainee); basic data; situation (current employer,
availability, mobility, interest in extras, shifts); licences (EASA, UK CAA, FAA, militar, A/B1/B2/C,
expiries, limitations — "el filtro más importante para empresas"); aircraft (A320, B737, ATR, Embraer,
business jets, helicopters); engines and skills (CFM56, LEAP, V2500, hydraulics, avionics, borescope,
structures); courses/documents (HF, EWIS, FTS, SMS, Part-145, approvals, type ratings); privacy (private,
anonymous, visible, open to opportunities, company block). 5.2 Pasaporte técnico is the main screen, "con
tarjetas y badges, no como un CV largo"; blocks: header, badges, experience timeline, documents, extra work
(days, target rate, countries, contract types), Candidate Pack. 5.3 Logbook: "Decisión de alcance — El
logbook será estrictamente personal. No se venderá como registro oficial, no sustituirá sistemas de empresa
ni procedimientos EASA/Part-145, y no incluirá validación por supervisor en esta fase." Fields: date,
aircraft, task type, environment (line/base/AOG/workshop), private description, optional attachments,
markers. 5.4 Wallet documental: categories; expiry alerts; granular control ("privado, visible para mí,
visible para empresas verificadas o compartido temporalmente"); "No compartir documentos por defecto;
siempre con autorización expresa del usuario."

**6. Herramientas (p.5).** Priority table: Logbook, Wallet, CV técnico (auto ES/EN), Modo extra work (72h,
weekend, rotation, AOG), Candidate Pack = "Muy alta"; Conversores, Ingresos extra simulator, Alertas =
"Alta"; Estimador neto = "Media-alta"; Calculadora fuel = "Media". Fuel note: kg = litros × densidad, user
enters real density from fuel slip, clear warning that it "no sustituye procedimientos oficiales".

**7. Información del perfil (p.6).** 7.1 Data categories: identity (name, surname, email, phone, country,
city/base, languages, LinkedIn), mobility (accepted countries, preferred airports, rotation,
local/national/Europe/world), preferences (temporary, permanent, freelance, AOG, weekend, nights, target
rate), privacy (visibility mode, hide employer, hide contact, block companies), licences (authority,
category, number, country, dates, limitations, type ratings), courses, experience (aircraft, engine,
system, maintenance type, years/months, level, role), documents. 7.2 Course blocks: safety/regulatory,
recurrent technical (EWIS, FTS, CDCCL, Part-145 procedures, continuation training), speciality,
operations/ramp, type/aircraft, documentation (OJT records).

**8. Privacidad, confianza y cumplimiento (p.6–7).** "El diseño debe ser privado por defecto y transparente
en cada uso de datos." 8.1 Five modes: Privado (company sees nothing), Anónimo ("Rol, país aproximado,
aviones, licencia y disponibilidad sin nombre ni empresa actual"), Visible verificado ("Perfil técnico para
empresas aprobadas, sin documentos privados por defecto"), Open to opportunities (appears in searches,
receives offers), Bloqueo de empresa. 8.2 Legal principles from the MVP: data protection by design and
minimisation; specific consent for WhatsApp/email/push alerts; "Explicar de forma sencilla cómo funciona el
matching y evitar decisiones laborales plenamente automatizadas sin revisión humana"; separate personal
logbook from official records; no default sharing of company-confidential documents; retention policy
(documents, applications, inactive profiles, account deletion). "Riesgo a evitar": never sell as a
substitute for Part-145 records, internal procedures, official licences or tax/labour advice.

**9. Oportunidades compatibles y avisos (p.7–8).** 9.1 Modo trabajo extra is "el puente entre utilidad y
negocio": 72 horas, Fin de semana, Rotación ("14/14, 21/21 u otras"), AOG ("Acepta urgencias con tarifa
superior"), Confidencial ("Recibe ofertas sin mostrar identidad hasta aceptar"). 9.2 Matching: "no debe ser
una caja negra ni una decisión automática de contratación. Debe ser un filtrado de compatibilidad: muestra
por qué una oferta encaja y qué requisitos faltan." Compared fields: licence (A, B1.1, B2, C, issuing
authority), aircraft ("experiencia declarada por familia/modelo"), engine (declared), courses (documents and
expiries), place (city, country, mobility, permits), dates ("disponibilidad real"), rate (accepted range or
target), language, privacy (contact allowed or anonymous invitation only). No weights or score formula.
9.3 WhatsApp as a key channel, "opt-in, plantillas aprobadas… y opción clara de desactivar". Example alert:
"Oferta compatible: B1 A320 Line Maintenance - Madrid - 3 días - 380 EUR/día. Match 91%. Opciones: Ver
oferta / Aplicar con Candidate Pack / No me interesa."

**10. Candidate Pack (p.8).** Generated when the technician decides to apply; reduces friction and HR
filtering. Sections: summary, offer requirements ("Qué cumple y qué falta"), licence (incl. "estado de
verificación"), relevant experience, courses, documents ("Solo documentos que el usuario autoriza a
compartir"), privacy, action (contact, interview, extra documentation, hiring). Process: the technician
"no debe 'enviar toda su vida' cada vez"; decides to apply, reviews what will be sent, authorises.

**11. Fase 2: empresas (p.8–9).** Opens to "empresas aeronáuticas verificadas: MRO, aerolíneas, CAMO,
operadores, empresas de staffing especializado, escuelas o centros de mantenimiento". 11.1 Company
onboarding: registration (company data, corporate domain, country, responsible contact), verification
("Documentación societaria, actividad aeronáutica, autorización para publicar"), internal roles ("RRHH,
mantenimiento, quality, manager, recruiter"), preferences (bases, profile types, contact methods, billing),
usage policy ("no contactar fuera de plataforma sin autorización, cumplimiento RGPD"). 11.2 Publishing a
need, example: B1.1 Licensed Engineer, A320 Family, CFM56/LEAP, Madrid, "3 días, incorporación viernes",
type "Line Maintenance / AOG / temporary support", requirements "Human Factors, EWIS, Fuel Tank Safety
válidos", rate "Rango diario u oferta cerrada", documents, visibility "Oferta pública, privada o solo
matching directo". 11.3 Company panel: active offers, received and suggested candidates, process states;
filters; comparable Candidate Packs; in-platform messaging with technician authorisation; hiring history.

**12. Modelo de negocio (p.9).** Keep technician registration free during acquisition; monetise companies
once there is critical mass. Lines and timing: Publicación de oferta (Fase 2), Suscripción empresa (Fase 2),
Success fee "Comisión si se cierra contratación o contrato temporal" (Fase 2/3, **no percentage given**),
AOG/urgente premium pack (Fase 2), Verificación documental "Servicio opcional de revisión/validación no
oficial" (Fase 2/3), Premium técnico optional (Fase 3), Datos agregados anonymised reports (Fase 3).
"Principio comercial: No cobrar pronto al técnico por registrarse."

**13. Roadmap (p.9–10).** 0 Demo (HTML móvil, PDF, feature list) → 1 MVP técnico (registration, passport,
wallet, logbook, privacy, extra work, "ofertas simuladas") → 2 Piloto de ofertas ("matching
manual/asistido", 10–20 companies/contacts, Candidate Pack) → 3 Empresa beta (company onboarding, publish,
candidates, messaging) → 4 Monetización (plans, billing, urgent packs, reporting) → 5 Escalado (languages,
indicative taxation, integrations, advanced verification). 13.1 Metrics: registered technicians, "Perfil
completado >70%", documents/courses added, extra-work mode activated, offers viewed/applied, interested
companies, "Tiempo hasta candidato válido".

**14. MVP visual (p.10).** 12 screens: Landing, Registro, Dashboard técnico, Pasaporte, Document Wallet,
Logbook, Extra Work, Ofertas compatibles, Candidate Pack, Herramientas, Privacidad, Empresa teaser.

**15. Riesgos y decisiones pendientes (p.10–11).** Sensitive data → GDPR by design, private by default;
company confidentiality → warn users not to upload proprietary docs; official logbook → keep personal, no
supervisor/official validation in MVP; taxation → indicative only; matching → "compatibilidad, no decisión
automática de contratación"; WhatsApp → opt-in/templates/opt-out; profile quality → "completitud,
verificaciones progresivas y revisión de documentos en fases futuras".

**16. Próximos pasos (p.11).** 1 close provisional name "JobSlice Aero / AeroCrew Passport"; 2 review mobile
demo with 5–10 real technicians; 3 define minimal registration form; 4 public landing with waiting list and
founding-member access; 5 acquisition messages for WhatsApp/LinkedIn/Instagram; 6 "Captar los primeros
50-100 perfiles técnicos manualmente"; 7 validate with 3–5 companies which Candidate Pack saves most time;
8 first pilot "inicialmente con matching manual asistido". "Decisión estratégica: La plataforma debe nacer
con mentalidad de marketplace, pero entrar en el mercado por el lado que aporta oferta: los técnicos."

**17. Referencias (p.11).** Disclaimer (not legal/tax/aeronautical advice). References: EASA Part-66 FAQ;
EASA Easy Access Rules, Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014 rev. Sept 2025 (Part-145, certifying staff); European
Commission GDPR principles and automated-decision/profiling restrictions; Meta/WhatsApp Business Platform
opt-in and templates; GOV.UK income tax 2026/27; Swiss Federal Tax Administration; ICAO SAF guide (Jet A-1
density 775–840 kg/m³ at 15 °C). "Nota final: El MVP debe enseñar la visión sin sobreprometer."
