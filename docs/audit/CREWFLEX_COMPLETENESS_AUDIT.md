## B. V1 — usable commercially end to end?

V1 is not a separately scoped document; the commercial loop is the capacity-first thesis of
`PRODUCT.md` (requirement → … → coverage). One transaction needs every step below; each step weighs the
same because missing any one of them stops the transaction.

**V1 verdict: NOT complete.** 7/12 steps `VERIFIED_LOCAL` (58 %), 0/12 `VERIFIED_PRODUCTION`. The product
today ends at an explained list of pseudonymous candidates: an organization **cannot contact, propose,
confirm or assign** anyone, and in production no new professional can ever be eligible (no verification
path). It is a working **capacity-and-matching core**, not yet a marketplace.

What V1.3 has that Crew Flex does not (MISSING / BLOCKED, grouped): document wallet and quarantine;
applications (9 states), proposals, confirmations, day coverage; Candidate Pack; recruiter workspace and
represented clients; tools hub (units, torque, durations, fuel), logbook; notifications; onboarding wizard;
password reset; organization verification and admin back office; AOG outreach and `aog` day state; shift
patterns; rates/logistics on requirements; rating restrictions, min years, countries, English.

What Crew Flex has that V1.3 lacks: real multi-tenant RLS, internal capacity/gap (V13-C18/C19 absent in
V1.3), deterministic explained eligibility with period-based expiry, DB-enforced invariants, idempotency,
bilingual UI.
